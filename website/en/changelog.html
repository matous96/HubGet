0.2 Nov 3, 2026

A new look for icons and search in Settings

Latest

Version 0.2.0.

New

  • A new look for icons – an iOS-style shape, with every icon in the same white tile, a subtle hint of its color and a soft shadow under the logo. Apps without an icon show their category symbol in its color.
  • Search in Settings (also Ctrl+F) – finds an option in every section, highlights the match, and toggles work right in the results. Accents don’t matter.
  • App banners and home page cards take on the app’s color – light in the light theme, dark in the dark theme. App of the Day follows the theme.

Improved

  • Operation history now lives in Settings, and older entries load a hundred at a time.
  • A new catalog downloaded in the background is applied once you pause for a moment – the window never freezes while you work. Search results never hold up typing, and HubGet uses less memory.
  • Installation errors show the actual cause from the package manager (such as the installer’s error code), not just “see the log for details”.

Fixed

  • The ChatGPT update kept being offered over and over – HubGet now says it will finish after a restart and stops offering it until then.
  • Screenshots of some apps (such as KiCad) didn’t load.
  • The banner icon on an app’s page sometimes stayed a placeholder tile.
0.1 Oct 21, 2026

An app store for Windows

First release

Versions 0.1.0 to 0.1.23 (Oct 21 – Nov 3, 2026) – from the first public preview to complete app management with WinGet, Chocolatey, Scoop and developer package managers.

New

  • A catalog of over 25,000 apps from WinGet, Chocolatey and Scoop – the Today section with collections and App of the day, categories, Top charts, Weekly risers and apps from the developer’s website.
  • Install, update and uninstall with WinGet, Chocolatey and Scoop – in bulk, with install options (silent, as administrator, for all users), SHA-256 verification and a fallback source when one fails.
  • Developer package managers – npm, Bun, pip, Cargo, vcpkg, .NET Tool and PowerShell in Developer mode. Missing Node.js, Python or Rust installs with one click.
  • Installed apps, including programs outside package managers, Microsoft Store apps and games from Steam and the EA app. Update all, ignored and snoozed updates.
  • Automatic updates on a schedule, with a minimum version age, exceptions for battery and metered connections, and confirmation for anything that needs admin rights.
  • Clean uninstall and install tracking – leftovers on disk and in the registry; HubGet closes running apps on its own before an operation.
  • Startup – programs and Store apps that start when you sign in to Windows, all in one place.
  • GitHub account – ratings, reviews and screenshots from the community, and sync of favorites, your package bundle and preferences across PCs.
  • Package bundles and profiles – move your apps to a new PC (JSON or a readable HTML page), collections by interest, and website links that open straight in HubGet.
  • Runs in the notification area with notifications, starting with Windows and a Windows 11-style window.
  • Seven languages, light and dark themes, a custom accent color, terminal commands, and a WinGet publishing wizard for developers.
  • The new name HubGet (formerly Unibox) and the hubget.eu website – the update moved your install over with all settings.

Improved

  • Fast startup from a saved catalog copy, smooth lists with thousands of apps, a catalog that refreshes while HubGet runs, and icons that load about four times faster.
